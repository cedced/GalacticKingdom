# ADR-004: Asset sourcing and generation policy

Status: Proposed (accept after the ship bake-off in the asset sprint fills in the image-to-3D choice below)

## Context

M0 and M1 art came from three places, none repeatable: an unknown-license
68-hull GLB pack (`assets/dump/3D_spaceships_pack/`), three user-provided
planet/sun renders of unknown origin, and five Recraft free-tier sprites
generated by hand and imported through `tools/import_body_sprite.py`. The
visual style is locked (pre-rendered painterly realism, ADR-003 and
`wiki/systems/rendering.md`), the intake tooling works, but there is no
answer for ships, VFX, UI icons, portraits, or audio, and provenance lives
in per-folder markdown tables that nothing checks.

Decided 2026-09-20 in a planning session (the question round is summarized
in `wiki/systems/asset-pipeline.md`).

## Decision

1. **Higgsfield is the generator for everything bespoke to our fiction.**
   Ships (concept art), planets, suns, stations, derelicts, VFX source clips,
   icons, portraits, and sound effects are generated through Higgsfield
   against the locked style block in `assets/ART_WORKFLOW.md`. Higgsfield's
   Terms of Use grant output ownership and commercial use on every plan, which
   clears the license-blocker class for generated assets.
2. **Envato Elements supplies non-diegetic material only**: music, stock
   VFX footage as raw material, textures/HDRIs, fonts, UI kits. No Envato
   ship, station, or planet enters `assets/` — style drift is the reason.
   Every download is registered to the Envato project "GalacticKingdom".
3. **Ships stay 3D (ADR-003 holds).** Hull geometry comes from
   image-to-3D on a Higgsfield concept sheet, then a Blender normalization
   pass, then GLB. The image-to-3D tool is chosen by a bake-off between
   Hunyuan3D (local, RTX 3080 10 GB) and Higgsfield's `image_to_3d` /
   `tripo_3d` on one concept; the winner is recorded here before acceptance:
   **image-to-3D tool: TBD (bake-off pending)**.
4. **Claude generates, the human curates.** Claude drives generation
   through the Higgsfield MCP server (`.mcp.json`, OAuth sign-in by the
   user), drops candidates in `assets/dump/gen/`, and the user approves
   before intake. Budget is the user's Higgsfield plan credits only; no
   other paid generation without a new decision.
5. **`data/assets/manifest.json` is the single source of truth** for every
   asset: wanted or present, provenance, prompt, model, seed, license. It
   is schema-validated and CI fails when a file under `assets/` has no
   manifest row. It replaces the per-folder `SOURCES.md` tables.
6. **License posture is case by case.** Unknown-license placeholders are
   replaced when a Higgsfield/Envato asset is both licensed and better, not
   on a deadline. Release exports print a license report; whether they
   should fail on unknown rows is an open question in the pipeline page.
7. **Cadence**: one asset sprint between M1 and M2 (tooling, ship pipeline
   proof, M2 batch), then every milestone plan carries its own asset rows.

## Consequences

- `assets/ART_WORKFLOW.md` stops describing a human-run generator; it
  describes what Claude does and what the user approves.
- A new `data/assets/` folder with schema, plus an assets check in
  `tools/validate_data.py` (or a sibling tool it calls). CI grows one step.
- Ships need three new tools: frame/concept intake, a Blender headless
  normalization script (`tools/normalize_hull.py`, run via Blender 5.2's
  Python), and a GLB check under the iso camera.
- VFX and rotation sheets need a video-to-frames tool (Python, bundled
  ffmpeg); the bodies pipeline's "N-angle sheets" open question gets a
  real experiment instead of a blocker.
- Envato's "not extractable from the end product" clause means release
  export presets use Godot's encrypted PCK once export presets exist (M2+).
- Higgsfield may train on generated content unless it is deleted from the
  account; whether to delete after intake is the user's call (open question).
- MCP-only generation means one job per call and no pre-flight cost query.
  Reproducibility comes from recording model, prompt, and seed in the
  manifest, not from scripts. If bulk sets become painful the Higgsfield
  CLI can be added later without changing this decision.
